# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback kindly, and don't harass anyone.

**Reporting:** email kartiksinghbisht1@gmail.com <!-- TODO(owner): change to support@triagefox.com once the domain is live -->. Reports are handled privately by the maintainer.

**Enforcement:** the maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
